House Conventions
Use 1/2 of minicans and 1/3 of regular cans for most built drinks; hand out whole cans/bottles only if straight-poured and the shift is slow
Liquor is always optional — shoot for 1.5oz, about a second of pouring
One pump of Torani syrup ≈ .25oz
Cream of coconut is standard for dirty sodas — small squeeze .5oz, big squeeze 1oz; offer creamer or rice milk if they don’t like coconut
After the order: read the recipe, ask listed options (ice, rim, ‘dirty’ or not)
Rotating iced teas live in 1g jugs — pour 6–8oz
Nightly ‘punch’ jug in red 10L dispensers — salt every jug, ~1.5 tsp per 10L batch, EXCEPT Whore-chata (Friday)
Straight cans/kombucha are available on request but don’t suggest them

Hard Reset — Watermelon-Mint Aqua Fresca
Built fresh today — no overnight steep needed.
Ingredients: Fresh watermelon (~2 melons, blended & strained) + fresh mint + agave + fresh lime, topped with STILL water (no tonic, no soda). 1.5 tsp salt.
LONGEST BUILD OF THE WEEK (~90 min) — start before service, not during
Cut 2 melons into chunks (~90oz juice/melon). Blend in batches with a handful of mint — the 12V blender is small, plan on 4–6 rounds
Strain rough through the fine mesh into a gallon container — some pulp is fine, this isn’t juice
Strained juice + agave + lime into the dispenser, STILL water to fill, 1.5 tsp salt, stir
Stir before each pour at service — pulp settles
Optional Blanco Tequila turns it into a watermelon margarita
Batch before service opens — never mid-rush. Label the dispenser.
